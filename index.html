Why some sites won't load directly.
frame-ancestors and X-Frame-Options are enforced by the browser before your page can intervene. A single HTML file cannot strip them — that requires a Service Worker in a separate sw.js on your own origin, plus a Wisp/WebSocket backend and a rewriter (Scramjet / Ultraviolet). Since browsers reject blob: and data: URLs for worker registration, it cannot be inlined here.

Workarounds in this file: Reader pulls clean text through a CORS-enabled extractor; Snapshot fetches raw HTML via a CORS proxy and renders it with scripts disabled. Both are read-only.
